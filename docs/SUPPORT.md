# Support Persistent Sage

Persistent Sage is **open source (MIT)**. Development is funded by the maintainer's time and community goodwill—not subscriptions or ads inside the app.

## Report bugs and request features

- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/g00siferdev-py/persistent-sage/issues)** — bugs, ideas, and general feedback (preferred)
- **Settings → General → Send feedback** — opens a prefilled issue from the app

## Voluntary donations

If Persistent Sage is useful to you, you can **optionally** support ongoing development (Daniel Greene / g00sifer Development Lab):

| Method | Link |
|--------|------|
| **PayPal** | [paypal.com/paypalme/g00sifer](https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/g00sifer) — QR in app donate panel |
| **Cash App** | [$DG9685](https://cash.app/$DG9685) — QR in app donate panel and onboarding |

Donations are **voluntary tips**. They do **not** unlock features, remove limits, or change app behavior. The app remains free and fully functional without donating.

### Microsoft Store installs

Microsoft Store policy allows **voluntary donations that provide no digital goods or services in return**, processed through a **third-party** payment page (PayPal, Cash App, etc.). Persistent Sage does **not** use Microsoft in-app purchase for donations.

When donating through a third-party site:

- **Microsoft is not the fundraiser or sponsor** of the contribution.
- Donations are between you and the maintainer; Microsoft does not process them.

If you enable third-party purchase links in Partner Center for Store submission, declare that the app may open an external browser or display a third-party payment QR for optional support links (no digital unlock).
